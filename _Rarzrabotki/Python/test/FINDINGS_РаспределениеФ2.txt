2. SALARY PERIOD IDENTIFICATION - CRITICAL FINDING
================================================================================

The salary period for a РаспределениеФ2 document is NOT determined by:
  ✗ Document.Дата (the date field)
  ✗ Document.Номер (the document number)

The salary period IS determined by:
  ✓ КассаДатаС    (Salary period start date - 1st of month)
  ✓ КассаДатаПо   (Salary period end date - last of month)

EXAMPLE - December 2025 Analysis:
  Document 033 (created 30.12.2025):
    • Document.Дата = 2025-12-30
    • КассаДатаС = 2025-12-01
    • КассаДатаПо = 2025-12-31
    → DECEMBER salary distribution (month 12)

  Document 028 (created 10.12.2025):
    • Document.Дата = 2025-12-10
    • КассаДатаС = 2025-11-01
    • КассаДатаПо = 2025-11-30
    → NOVEMBER salary distribution (month 11)

EXPLANATION:
Both documents have December dates, but they refer to different salary periods:
- Doc 033 was created late in December to process December payroll
- Doc 028 was created early in December to process November payroll

================================================================================

1. PERIOD DETERMINATION:
   When querying РаспределениеФ2, always filter by КассаДатаС/КассаДатаПо,
   NOT by Document.Дата. The creation date may be days or weeks after the
   salary period ends.

2. DOCUMENT PURPOSE:
   РаспределениеФ2 distributes salary amounts to employees/departments.
   It processes:
   - Full month salary
   - Partial period salary (e.g., 10/1-10/15, 10/16-10/31)
   - Salary adjustments and corrections

3. EMPLOYEES & AMOUNTS:
   - Сотрудники section: basic salary by employee
   - Распределение section: detailed distribution by employee & department

4. CASH FLOW TRACKING:
   - БДДС register has РаспределениеФ2 as Регистратор (registrar)
   - Each salary entry creates БДДС records
   - Tracks salary by СтатьяДвиженияДенежныхСредств (salary categories)

5. TIMING CONSIDERATIONS:
   - Documents can be created multiple times per salary period (adjustments)
   - Documents can be created after the salary period ends (corrections)
   - Always use КассаДатаС/КассаДатаПо to identify the actual period

6. PARTIAL PERIODS:
   Some documents cover partial months (e.g., 10/1-10/15 vs 10/16-10/31)
   This allows for:

   - Period-specific calculations
